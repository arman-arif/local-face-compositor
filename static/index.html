    Privacy and data
    • Images are processed on this machine. The app doesn't call external AI APIs.
    • InsightFace may download its face-analysis pack at first startup unless it's already installed. For fully offline use, install all model assets in advance.
    • Uploads are deleted after each run. Results stay in data/outputs/ until you delete them.
    • Don't use this app to impersonate or defraud anyone, or to create non-consensual intimate imagery.
